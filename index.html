Our Story

A Garden Built Around Sri Lanka's Natural Heritage

Manju Spice & Herbal Garden is a visitor-oriented spice and herbal garden nestled in the Koggala–Habaraduwa area of southern Sri Lanka.

We introduce guests to locally cultivated herbs and spices, sharing insight into their cultivation, their place in traditional Ayurvedic knowledge, their culinary uses, and their role in natural wellness.
